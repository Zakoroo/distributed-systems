# Design decisions

Parent: [Knowledge base](../README.md).

Decision records explain why the group selected an approach. They do not replace
official assignment requirements or general concept notes.

1. [0001: Collaboration framework](0001-collaboration-framework.md): Markdown,
   a shared knowledge index, and a human/agent-editable backlog.
2. [0002: Lab 1 server and locking](0002-lab1-server-and-locking.md): ordered
   routing, middleware, caller-owned validation, and generic keyed locking.
3. [0003: Shared server and Lab 1 packages](0003-shared-server-and-lab1-packages.md):
   the reusable `httpserver` module, application adapters, middleware, and file policy.
4. [0004: Lab 1 source and docs](0004-lab1-source-and-docs.md): `src/` and `docs/`
   folders, stable module/agent-guide placement, and updated build commands.

Use the next available number for a significant new decision. State whether it
is proposed or accepted, its context, alternatives, and consequences. Mark a
decision superseded when a later accepted choice replaces it.
